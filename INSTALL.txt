SETUP — BACKBURNER MUSIC PAGE

1. In your local rffadhil repository, create a folder named: music
2. Copy index.html into that folder. Final path: music/index.html
3. Commit and push the change to GitHub.
4. On GitHub, open repository rffadhil/rffadhil > Settings > Pages.
5. Under Build and deployment, choose "Deploy from a branch".
6. Select branch "main" and folder "/ (root)", then click Save.
7. Wait for GitHub Pages to finish publishing. The page should be at:
   https://rffadhil.github.io/rffadhil/music/
8. Copy the snippet from README-snippet.md into the profile README.md.

IMPORTANT:
- This page uses Spotify's official Embed iframe. Visitors can press Play on the embedded player.
- Spotify controls playback; login/account/region/browser restrictions may apply.
- Do not replace the encrypted-media permission on the iframe, because Spotify says it is needed for full playback rather than preview-only behavior.
